✧ DISEASE EXPLORER

Understand Diseases

Explore common diseases related to major human organs, their causes, symptoms and prevention.

❤️
HEART

Hypertension

Cause: Persistently high blood pressure.

Symptoms: Often no noticeable symptoms; severe cases may cause headache or dizziness.

Prevention: Regular exercise, balanced diet, less salt and regular check-ups.

🫁
LUNGS

Asthma

Cause: Inflammation and narrowing of the airways.

Symptoms: Wheezing, coughing, chest tightness and breathing difficulty.

Prevention: Avoid known triggers and follow prescribed treatment.

🧠
BRAIN

Stroke

Cause: Interruption of blood supply to part of the brain.

Symptoms: Sudden weakness, speech difficulty, confusion or loss of balance.

Prevention: Control blood pressure, exercise and maintain a healthy lifestyle.

🫘
KIDNEY

Kidney Stones

Cause: Crystals or mineral deposits forming in the urinary system.

Symptoms: Severe side or back pain, painful urination and sometimes blood in urine.

Prevention: Drink enough water and maintain a balanced diet.

🟤
LIVER

Fatty Liver

Cause: Excess fat accumulation in liver cells.

Symptoms: May have no symptoms; some people may experience tiredness or discomfort.

Prevention: Healthy weight, physical activity and balanced food habits.

🩸
BLOOD

Anaemia

Cause: Reduced healthy red blood cells or haemoglobin.

Symptoms: Tiredness, weakness, pale skin and shortness of breath.

Prevention: Adequate iron and nutrient-rich foods and medical advice when needed.
